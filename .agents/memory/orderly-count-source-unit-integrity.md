---
name: Orderly count source unit integrity
description: Why a matching historical count number or value cannot certify its saved unit, and what must remain atomic at creation.
---

Do not treat a matching source total, pack arithmetic, or dollar reconciliation as permission to label an Orderly count in the item's current unit. Dated tier labels and pack geometry must establish the source unit; a verified difference or uninterpretable Case/Pack basis blocks new session creation rather than guessing a conversion. Existing historical drafts and immutable sessions are separate operator-review work, not automatic repair targets.

**Why:** Bay Hill had numerically matching quantities and values saved under a different unit label. Independent review also found that a correct preview can become unsafe if its item unit, source evidence, date, or duplicate-session state changes before creation commits.

**How to apply:** Keep preview findings actionable, and at creation revalidate the exact source evidence, item unit identity, approved batch metadata, and duplicate-session state while the relevant rows are locked through the write. An operator's decision about canonical units or historical correction remains separate; never multiply historical quantities merely to make the labels match.
