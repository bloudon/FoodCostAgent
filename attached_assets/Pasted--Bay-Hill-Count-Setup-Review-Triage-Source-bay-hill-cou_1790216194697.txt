### Reading these

**Broccoli — `verified`.** 15 invoice lines, most recent 17 Aug, consistently `1/20 POUND`. The `1/1 Case` label resolves to 20 lb. Strongest evidence in the set.

**Shallots, Zucchini, Apricot, Cabbage-Red — `verified` for the invoiced pack.** Each matches one side of the retained conflict; the other side is historical. Shallots and Zucchini run to 17 Aug, so current.

**Celery — `conflicting`, and genuinely so.** Harvill's invoices show **both** `1/18 EACH` and `1/36 EACH` under the same SKU, while the catalog retains `1/15 EA | 1/36 EA`. Three distinct figures. *Question: does Harvill's ship 18 or 36 per case today, and what is the 15?*

**Juice - Lemon — `conflicting`.** Invoice says `1/32 OUNCE`; catalog retains `12/1 QT | 4/1 GAL`. A 32-oz bottle is not either. *Question: is the lemon juice bought as single quarts now?*

**CHAYOTE — `insufficient evidence`.** One line, 15 May, no recent activity.
