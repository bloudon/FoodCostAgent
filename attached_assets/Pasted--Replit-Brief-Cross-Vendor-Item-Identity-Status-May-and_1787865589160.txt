## P1 — Item identity is decided by pack compatibility (requires re-seed)

`periodComparison` after June: **57 added, 39 removed**. All 39 removals pair by name with an addition — nothing left inventory. **39 items forked into duplicates**, with May's history on one record and June's on another. Only 18 additions are genuinely new products.

Examples (different `inventoryItemId` on each side):

| May | June |
|---|---|
| `Avocado` | `Avocado` |
| `Arugula` | `Arugula — 1 × 1.5 × 1 LB` |
| `Celery` | `Celery — 1 × 15 × 1 EA` |
| `Beef - Ground Chuck` | `Beef - Ground Chuck — 1 × 120 × 1 EA` |

### Root cause

The review flow offers only two outcomes: **link** (requires normalized packs to match) or **create a separate inventory item**. A product supplied by two vendors in different pack sizes always fails the link test and forks.

The distinction that matters is not captured:

| Situation | Correct outcome |
|---|---|
| Same vendor, genuinely different pack (Casamigos 5×50 ML minis vs full bottles; Milk-Whole 1 GAL vs 4 GAL) | Separate item — different physical product form |
| **Different vendor, different pack** (Avocado `1/24 EA` from Mr. Green's vs `1/48 EA` from Harvill's) | **Same item, second vendor record** |

The vendor is already on the row and is the signal that separates these.

### Why it matters beyond tidiness

The multi-vendor population is **92 products across three months, $22,619.98**, concentrated in produce and dry goods. Cross-vendor price comparison depends on one inventory item holding several vendor records — fragmenting the item makes the comparison impossible. Verified from the source data that the comparison is meaningful:

* Asparagus: Harvill's $3.09/LB vs Mr. Green's $5.56/LB
* Avocado: Harvill's $0.80/EA (1/48) vs Mr. Green's $1.93/EA (1/24)
* Lays chips: Sysco $0.68/EA (1/60) vs Cheney $1.56/EA (1/30)

### Requested

1. **A third review outcome: link to the existing item as a different vendor's pack** — one `inventory_item`, a new `vendor_item` carrying that vendor's geometry and price.
2. **Default cross-vendor pack differences to linking, not forking**, when name matches and vendor differs.
3. **Surface it in the Category 2 bulk dialog** — flag which proposed variants already exist under another vendor, so an operator sees "this will create a second Avocado" before confirming.

### Questions to answer first

* **Does the import populate `vendor_items` at all?** After May, `vendorSkus` was empty on all 3,052 items. If a cross-vendor link does not create a vendor record, the second vendor's pack and price are silently lost — worse than the fork.
* **Which of the 30 Category 2 decisions are genuinely different products?** Casamigos/Ketel One minis and Milk-Whole gallon tiers should stay separate; the produce items should not. That split determines the correct default.

## P3 — Parser: recover units the source already provides

Per month, ~66 rows carry unsupported base units that are real measurable units or self-describing:

| Unit | May rows | Note |
|---|---|---|
| `GR` | 17 | grams |
| `PT` | 2 | pint |
| `Cup` | 1 | volume |
| `KEG 5.16G` | 19 | self-describing: 5.16 gal |
| `#10` | 18 | standard foodservice can |

Separately, `N/M Case` rows carry real multipliers that are currently discarded. Orderly stores Default Pack Size and Default UOM as distinct fields, so `12/10 Case` means 12 × 10 = 120 units. Price arithmetic confirms it independently:

| Item | Pack | Units | Price | Per unit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Captain Morgan Mini | 12/10 | 120 | $117.60 | $0.98 |
| Bacardi Mini | 12/10 | 120 | $122.40 | $1.02 |
| Tanqueray Mini | 12/10 | 120 | $200.40 | $1.67 |
| Titos Mini | 12/5 | 60 | $117.80 | $1.96 |
| Casamigos Mini | 5/12 | 60 | $251.00 | $4.18 |

Contrast `Crown Royal Mini` — `1/50` with UOM `Milliliter` — which parses fine today. Same information content; the only difference is that `Milliliter` is on the supported list and `Case` is not. Recommend keeping `1/1 Case` opaque (no multipliers to recover) but recovering `N/M <container>` as a count.

**Note:** this changes what lands in the catalog, so it must ship before the re-seed, not after.
