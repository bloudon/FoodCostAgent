Questions worth resolving before build

1. Is staleness per-decision or per-batch? #1247 specifies "an integrity fingerprint of the source batch and preview state." If that fingerprint is preview-wide, a single catalog change invalidates every saved decision at once. That's not hypothetical during a seed: approving May mutates the catalog June is compared against, so all saved June decisions could go stale in one event. Staleness should key to the specific candidate identity each decision targets, not the whole preview.

2. How do bulk actions save? #1245 step 2 specifies endpoints that save "one pending batch decision at a time." The bulk button creates 31 decisions in a single click. Thirty-one sequential calls is slow and has a partial-failure state — if #17 fails, what does the reviewer see, and what's persisted? This needs either a batch endpoint or explicit partial-failure UX.

3. Does the footer count reflect saved or optimistic state? Our whole verification method leans on that count, and it's what caught a wrong row in a prior session. If it decrements on click before the save confirms, it stops being a reliable check.

4. Two reviewers at once. #1245 anticipates "a different authorized reviewer resumes the batch" — sequential handoff. Simultaneous editing looks like last-write-wins. Is there a lock or conflict detection?

5. Is Approve a single transaction? #1245 step 4 mentions "the existing approval transaction," which hints atomicity already exists — worth confirming outright, along with timeout behavior on 5,409 rows. None of the three tickets validate Approve, and it's still the step nobody has observed.

One expectation to set

#1247's out-of-scope line — "Replaying decisions against a different month, environment, company, or source-property catalog" — is correct, and matches the month-interdependence finding in the runbook. But it means the manifest will not carry dev decisions onto production. That transfer stays human: the runbook plus the decision log, re-derived against production's own catalog state. Worth saying plainly now so nobody plans the seed expecting to export from dev and import to prod.
