# FnB Cost Pro Subscription & Plan Realignment

## Decision Summary

FnB Cost Pro will launch with one complete core platform rather than traditional Starter, Pro, and Enterprise feature tiers.

The public offering will present three customer paths:

1. **Single Location**
2. **Multi-Location**
3. **Enterprise Operations**

Technically, the billing model only needs two subscription types:

* **Platform**
* **Enterprise**

Multi-Location is the standard Platform subscription with additional licensed locations and the related cross-location capabilities enabled.

## Core Commercial Principle

> **Every core capability is included from day one. Pricing grows with the scale and complexity of the operation—not through an artificial ladder of locked features.**

A sophisticated independent restaurant should not have to upgrade to access vendor comparison, predictive ordering, theoretical food cost, variance reporting, or advanced inventory tools.

The distinction is operational:

* One location operates as a single-location business.
* Two or more locations require connected multi-location tools.
* Complex clubs, resorts, hotels, and multi-outlet organizations require tailored Enterprise configuration.

# Public Plan Structure

## Single Location

For an independent restaurant or one operating location.

Includes the complete FnB Cost Pro platform:

* Recipe and sub-recipe costing
* Theoretical food cost
* Actual-versus-theoretical variance reporting
* AI-assisted menu, recipe, invoice, inventory, and spreadsheet capture
* Inventory management
* Multiple storage areas within the location
* Mobile inventory counting
* Shelf and catch-weight counting
* Vendor management
* Vendor price comparison
* Vendor connectivity
* Predictive ordering
* Purchase orders and receiving
* Order guides
* Prep and production planning
* POS sales imports
* QuickBooks integration
* Standard roles and permissions
* Smart dashboards and reporting

### Important definition

A single operating location may contain many internal storage locations, such as:

* Walk-in
* Freezer
* Dry storage
* Bar
* Prep kitchen
* Wine storage

These are not separately billable locations.

The message should be:

> **One location. One price. Every core tool included.**

---

## Multi-Location

For operators managing two or more separate restaurant locations.

Includes everything in Single Location, plus:

* Multiple licensed operating locations
* Company-wide and location-specific dashboards
* Inventory transfers between locations
* Cross-location inventory visibility
* Consolidated reporting
* Location performance comparisons
* Shared recipe management
* Shared menu management
* Location-specific recipes and pricing where needed
* Location-specific vendor accounts and pricing
* Central purchasing visibility
* Cross-location permissions and oversight
* Multi-location predictive ordering

Multi-Location is not a different or “better” version of the product. It is the organizational layer required when multiple operating locations need to work together.

Adding the second licensed location activates the multi-location capabilities.

---

## Enterprise Operations

For complex food-and-beverage organizations requiring tailored configuration and implementation.

Target organizations include:

* Clubs
* Resorts
* Hotels
* Multi-outlet properties
* Large restaurant groups
* Commissary operations
* Franchise organizations
* Central production kitchens

Enterprise may include:

* Multiple outlets within one property
* Central storerooms serving several outlets
* Outlet and storage-location hierarchies
* Central purchasing and replenishment
* Custom approval workflows
* Advanced permissions and governance
* Organization-wide recipe and menu standards
* Specialized integrations
* Existing-system migration
* Custom analytics and reporting
* Tailored implementation
* Training and operational advisory support
* Negotiated service and support requirements

Enterprise is defined by **operational complexity**, not simply by the number of locations.

A straightforward five-location restaurant group may use Multi-Location. A single resort with ten outlets, central storage, banquets, and custom workflows may require Enterprise.

# Recommended Billing Model

## Subscription products

Technically support:

```text
platform
enterprise
```

Do not maintain separate `single_location` and `multi_location` subscription products unless Stripe or billing requirements make that necessary.

## Platform subscription

Includes:

* Full core product
* One licensed operating location
* Vendor connectivity
* Standard support

## Additional locations

Each additional licensed operating location is billed separately.

When the account has two or more licensed locations, multi-location capabilities become available automatically.

## Guided implementation

Guided Implementation remains a separate one-time service rather than a feature tier.

It may include:

* Importing existing inventory
* Importing recipes
* Importing vendor catalogs and pricing
* POS data onboarding
* Storage-location setup
* Data validation
* Exception review
* Training
* Initial operational configuration

## Enterprise

Enterprise uses custom pricing and scope.

# Internal Subscription Model

Recommended company fields or equivalent:

```ts
subscription_plan: "platform" | "enterprise";
subscription_status:
  | "trialing"
  | "active"
  | "past_due"
  | "canceled"
  | "incomplete";

billing_interval: "monthly" | "annual";
licensed_location_count: number;
stripe_customer_id: string | null;
subscription_current_period_end: Date | null;
```

Trial is a subscription status, not a plan.

## Derived operating mode

```ts
function getOperatingMode(company) {
  if (company.subscription_plan === "enterprise") {
    return "enterprise";
  }

  if (company.licensed_location_count >= 2) {
    return "multi_location";
  }

  return "single_location";
}
```

This avoids requiring a plan change when a customer adds another location.

# Required Cleanup Before Launch

Because there are no live customers, remove the old tier structure cleanly rather than preserving backward compatibility.

## Retire these internal keys

```text
free
basic
pro
```

Replace them with:

```text
platform
enterprise
```

There is no need for a Legacy plan.

Internal development or test accounts should use explicit test flags or administrative subscription overrides rather than a fake public tier.

## Consolidate plan configuration

Pricing and entitlement data should not remain split between:

* `pricing-constants.ts`
* `tier-config.ts`
* marketing-page constants
* onboarding comparison data
* Stripe mapping files

Create one authoritative plan catalog on the server.

Example:

```ts
type PlanDefinition = {
  key: "platform" | "enterprise";
  displayName: string;
  public: boolean;
  monthlyPrice?: number;
  annualPrice?: number;
  includedLocations: number | null;
  stripeLookupKeys?: {
    monthly?: string;
    annual?: string;
  };
  capabilities: FeatureKey[];
};

export const PLAN_CATALOG: Record<string, PlanDefinition> = {
  platform: {
    key: "platform",
    displayName: "FnB Cost Pro Platform",
    public: true,
    monthlyPrice: 228,
    annualPrice: 198,
    includedLocations: 1,
    stripeLookupKeys: {
      monthly: "fnb_platform_monthly",
      annual: "fnb_platform_annual",
    },
    capabilities: CORE_PLATFORM_CAPABILITIES,
  },

  enterprise: {
    key: "enterprise",
    displayName: "Enterprise Operations",
    public: true,
    includedLocations: null,
    capabilities: [
      ...CORE_PLATFORM_CAPABILITIES,
      ...MULTI_LOCATION_CAPABILITIES,
      ...ENTERPRISE_CAPABILITIES,
    ],
  },
};
```

Also define additional-location Stripe prices centrally:

```ts
export const ADDITIONAL_LOCATION_PRICING = {
  monthly: {
    amount: 149,
    lookupKey: "fnb_additional_location_monthly",
  },
  annual: {
    amount: 129,
    lookupKey: "fnb_additional_location_annual",
  },
};
```

Marketing, billing, onboarding, upgrade flows, and feature gates should consume this shared source of truth or an API generated from it.

# Stripe Product Structure

Recommended Stripe prices:

```text
fnb_platform_monthly
fnb_platform_annual
fnb_additional_location_monthly
fnb_additional_location_annual
```

Enterprise remains custom and may use manually created subscriptions, quotes, or negotiated Stripe prices.

The Stripe subscription should use:

* One Platform base-price item
* Zero or more additional-location quantity items

Example:

```text
Platform monthly × 1
Additional location monthly × 3
```

This represents four total licensed locations.

# Acceptance Criteria for the Realignment

* Old `free`, `basic`, and `pro` plan keys are removed.
* `platform` and `enterprise` are the only subscription-plan values.
* Trial is represented only through subscription status.
* One Platform subscription includes one licensed location.
* Additional locations are billed as a recurring quantity.
* Multi-location capabilities activate at two licensed locations.
* Core capabilities are available to all paid Platform customers.
* Storage locations within one restaurant do not count as billable operating locations.
* Enterprise is determined through subscription and configuration, not only location count.
* Pricing, Stripe lookup keys, feature entitlements, onboarding, and marketing use one authoritative plan catalog.
* The in-app Account page replaces the locked-feature upgrade grid.
* English and Spanish pricing and subscription messaging remain aligned.
* No page refers to Starter or Pro at launch.

## Final Product Principle

> **FnB Cost Pro is one complete platform. The customer pays more only when the operation adds locations or requires a more complex, tailored organizational model.**
