# Bay Hill Initial Import Duplicate Remediation

## What & Why

Repair duplicate FnB inventory-item identities created by the original Bay Hill May/June Orderly imports after #1108 has fixed the importer.

The goal is to consolidate accidental core-item splits while preserving all historical count/location evidence and source provenance.

## Preconditions

- #1108 is complete and green.
- Forensics has identified affected duplicate groups and downstream references.
- No additional historical months have been loaded.

## Done looks like

- Every confirmed accidental duplicate group resolves to one canonical FnB inventory item.
- All safe downstream references are repointed to the canonical item.
- May/June location-level count evidence and quantities remain intact.
- External mappings resolve to the canonical item only.
- No duplicate valuation remains from the accidentally split core records.
- Ambiguous groups remain unresolved/reviewable and are not auto-merged.
- No historical source evidence is deleted or rewritten.
- May and June preview/re-import behavior resolves consistently through the corrected #1108 logic.
- Reconciled monthly inventory totals remain unchanged except for removal of duplicate identity effects.

## Required remediation rules

### Canonical item selection

Choose the canonical record based on evidence, including:

- authoritative external mapping
- downstream historical references
- completeness of item metadata
- existing store/location usage

Do not choose by display name alone.

### Safe merge behavior

For a confirmed same-item split:

- repoint dependent foreign keys to the canonical inventoryItemId
- consolidate external mappings
- preserve separate location assignments
- preserve separate count-session/source rows
- prevent duplicate store-inventory rows
- deactivate or mark the duplicate item as merged if hard deletion is unsafe

### Do not merge automatically when

- source identity is ambiguous
- pack/UOM evidence suggests genuinely different physical products
- conflicting external mappings exist
- downstream references cannot be safely repointed

Return those cases for review.

- rewriting source workbooks
